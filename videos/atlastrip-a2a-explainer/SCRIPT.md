# SCRIPT — atlastrip-a2a-explainer

**Voice:** Kokoro (local), male, measured
**Voice direction:** An engineer explaining something they built, to another engineer.
Unhurried. Slight downward inflection on the claims, because they are claims, not
pitches. Never bright, never salesy. Let the numbers sit.

---

## Line 1 — Five agents, no shared runtime (Frame 1)

**Time:** 0.0 – 6.0s
**Delivery:** Flat and certain. A beat before the third sentence; it is the turn.

    Five AI agents. Five different frameworks. And they cannot import each other.

## Line 2 — The constraint is real (Frame 2)

**Time:** 6.0 – 16.0s
**Delivery:** Matter of fact. "Cannot exist" is a statement of fact, not a complaint.

    That's not a design choice. CrewAI pins one version of the OpenAI client.
    Pydantic AI pins another. A shared environment cannot exist.

**Time:** 16.0 – 26.0s
**Delivery:** The two middle sentences are a pair; give them equal weight and a
clean gap between them. Land hard on "a task, with a lifecycle."

    So the agents share a protocol instead. MCP gives an agent its tools.
    A2A lets agents give each other work. Not a function call. A task, with a lifecycle.

**Time:** 76.0 – 85.0s
**Delivery:** "Not fails." is its own sentence and its own beat. Then let the

**Time:** 85.0 – 93.0s
